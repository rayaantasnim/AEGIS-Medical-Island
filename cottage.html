Architectural Blueprint

North Coast Archipelago & Central Eco-Lounge

Overview: 20 Overwater Villas & Radial Wooden Bridge Network

Arranged circularly over pristine blue waters just outside the main beach on the North Coast, this zone comprises 20 independent Overwater Villas built on private micro-islands . At the geometric center of this circle floats a two-story Central Eco-Lounge supported by underwater pillars, connected to each villa via suspension wooden bridges styled in a precise radial design .

  • Radial Bridge Network: Wooden hanging bridges spreading like wheel spokes from the central lounge to each villa.
  • Central Social Pavilion: Two-story sustainably sourced timber lounge hosting live entertainment and floating dining .
  • Absolute Marine Isolation: Pristine ocean setting ensuring complete mental tranquility and privacy.

The overwater residential zone represents the pinnacle of sustainable luxury engineering within AEGIS Island Medical. By positioning twenty independent villas in a circular formation around a communal eco-lounge, the architecture fosters a harmonious balance between serene isolation and vibrant community engagement. Pedestrian transit across the suspended wooden hanging bridges preserves a quiet, zero-emission eco-environment, allowing guests to absorb the tranquil coastal sounds and breathtaking oceanic horizons without mechanical disruption .
