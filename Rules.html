1. Communication & Technology Restrictions

RULE 01 Prohibition of Digital Devices

The use of all personal communication devices is strictly prohibited during your stay at the complex. The governing authority designates this island as an absolute natural sanctuary intended for complete mental tranquility, unhindered by external digital noise, notifications, or continuous online connections. Operating under strict zero-emission and signal-shielding standards, the complex enforces a complete blackout on personal electronic transmissions to ensure that guests experience deep psychological relaxation and uncompromised privacy. All personal cellular hardware, tablets, laptops, and peripheral wireless gadgets must be surrendered upon initial arrival at the reception threshold. Any attempt to introduce unauthorized signal-broadcasting hardware into private suites or shared public areas will trigger immediate automated security protocols and lead to the formal cancellation of residency privileges.

RULE 02 Lobby Storage of Personal Devices

Upon crossing the threshold of the facility, clients must surrender all mobile phones, tablets, smart devices, and laptops at the Main Lobby reception. These items will be safely held in an ultra-secure, climate-controlled vault and will be returned intact only upon formal checkout and departure. Each surrendered device is cataloged using encrypted biometric identifiers and placed within individual Faraday-shielded storage compartments to prevent remote data access or electronic tracking. The main lobby vault is continuously guarded by Elite Tactical Security personnel and monitored by server-less biometric authorization systems. Guests are provided with physical, serialized custody receipts guaranteeing the absolute physical safety and digital isolation of their hardware throughout the entire duration of their stay.

RULE 03 Confiscation of Unauthorized Cameras

Possessing any personal cameras, hidden recording apparatus, or spy gadgets inside the perimeter of the island is a severe, punishable offense. Any unauthorized imaging or audio recording technology detected by security layers will be permanently confiscated without eligibility for return. The island incorporates non-stop optical and frequency scanner arrays designed to pinpoint lens reflections, hidden micro-chips, and active recording signals across all public and private zones. In the event an unauthorized camera device is identified, tactical security teams are dispatched immediately to secure the hardware. The confiscated equipment will undergo immediate destructive data purging under sovereign protocol, and the offending party will face immediate legal proceedings under the non-disclosure agreements signed prior to entry.

RULE 04 Smartwatches and Wearable Technology

Clients are explicitly forbidden from wearing any smartwatches, fitness trackers, or bluetooth-enabled accessories that contain embedded SIM cards or maintain independent internet connectivity. Only traditional, fully mechanical timepieces without wireless transmission capabilities are permitted. This restriction ensures that ambient telemetry scanning matrices operate without radio-frequency interference from unauthorized consumer wearables. Upon check-in, all wearable items are subjected to electromagnetic diagnostic scans. If a guest requires continuous physiological monitoring, the hospital authority will issue specialized, air-gapped bio-telemetry bands that interface directly with the localized server-less AI Control Room, ensuring absolute medical oversight without compromising the sanctuary's strict zero-wireless protocol.

RULE 05 Satellite Communications Clearance

All long-range communication gear, including satellite phones and multi-channel wireless walkie-talkies aboard private yachts arriving from international waters, must be declared, powered down, and securely locked at the Elite Marina Gate before any passenger steps onto the docks. Customs officers apply specialized physical seals to all onboard communication consoles prior to granting maritime clearance. Vessel captains must maintain these seals intact throughout their stay in sovereign waters. Any unauthorized transmission detected originating from anchored vessels will result in the immediate deployment of tactical maritime interception craft and potential expulsion of the vessel from the island’s territorial perimeter.
